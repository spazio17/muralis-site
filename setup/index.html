Turn the battery limits off

Two minutes, once, on the tablet. Muralis sets up everything else by itself.

Why you must

On battery with the screen off, tablets stop background apps. Yours can wake showing the launcher instead of your dashboard.

Why Muralis cannot

Android puts this behind a dialog only you can accept. No app is allowed to grant it to itself.

  1. Settings › Apps › Muralis › BatteryChoose Unrestricted.

On older Android the same setting lives under Settings, Apps, Special access, Battery optimisation, where Muralis should be set to Do not optimise.

  1. Settings › Apps › Special access › Battery optimisationSwitch the list to All apps, find Muralis, choose Do not optimise.
  2. Settings › Battery › App launch › MuralisTurn Manage automatically off, then turn on all three switches.

Muralis is missing from App launch when it is the home app on that tablet, which is how a provisioned panel is set up. That is expected: the list is for apps that start themselves in the background, and the home app is started by Android. Step one is the one that matters.

  1. Settings › Apps › Manage apps › MuralisTurn Autostart on.
  2. Same screen › Battery saverChoose No restrictions.
  1. Settings › Apps › Muralis › BatteryChoose Unrestricted.
  2. Settings › Battery › Background usage limitsTake Muralis out of Sleeping apps.
  1. Settings › Battery › App battery management › MuralisAllow background running.

These paths are right for most tablets, but makers rename screens and move them between Android versions: what is Battery on one device is App battery usage on another. If a step is not where it says, check your maker's own support pages, or dontkillmyapp.com, which keeps the steps per maker and per Android version, with screenshots.

Check it worked

Unplug the tablet, press the power button so the screen goes dark, wait ten minutes, then press it again. Your dashboard should be on the screen.
