Setup

Two settings only you can change

Two minutes, once, on the tablet. Muralis sets up everything else by itself.

New tablet? Set it up by QR first, on the Download page.

  1. Battery limits offNeeded Your page is still there after the tablet sleeps.
  2. BrightnessOptional Muralis and your page can set it.

1. Turn the battery limits off

On battery, with the screen off, tablets stop apps in the background. The tablet can then wake on its launcher instead of your page.

Steps for your tablet maker

  1. Settings › Apps › Muralis › BatteryChoose Unrestricted.

Older Android: Settings, Apps, Special access, Battery optimisation. Set Muralis to Do not optimise.

  1. Settings › Apps › Special access › Battery optimisationSwitch the list to All apps, find Muralis, choose Do not optimise.
  2. Settings › Battery › App launch › MuralisTurn Manage automatically off, then turn on all three switches.
Muralis is not in App launch?

That is expected when Muralis is the home app, as on a panel set up by QR. App launch lists apps that start themselves in the background, and Android starts the home app itself. Step 1 is the one that matters.

  1. Settings › Apps › Manage apps › MuralisTurn Autostart on.
  2. Same screen › Battery saverChoose No restrictions.
  1. Settings › Apps › Muralis › BatteryChoose Unrestricted.
  2. Settings › Battery › Background usage limitsTake Muralis out of Sleeping apps.
  1. Settings › Battery › App battery management › MuralisAllow background running.
A step is not where it says?

Makers rename screens and move them between Android versions. Battery on one tablet is App battery usage on another. Look at your maker's support pages, or at dontkillmyapp.com: it has the steps for each maker and Android version, with screenshots.

Why can Muralis not do this itself?

Android lets only a person turn the battery limits off. No app can do it for itself, not even as the tablet's admin.

Check that it worked

  1. Unplug the tablet.
  2. Press the power button. The screen goes dark.
  3. Wait ten minutes.
  4. Press it again. Your page should be on the screen.

2. Let Muralis set the brightness

Optional. Without it, the brightness controls in Muralis and in your page do nothing, and the tablet keeps its own brightness. Everything else works.

  1. Muralis settings › DisplayUnder Brightness, tap Grant it now. Allow Muralis on the Android screen that opens. Muralis comes back by itself.
By hand, or over a cable

On the tablet: Settings, Apps, Special access, Modify system settings, then allow Muralis. Some makers keep that list under Settings, Apps, Advanced, Special app access.

Over USB with adb: adb shell appops set org.spazio17.muralis WRITE_SETTINGS allow

A few makers hide this screen. Then Grant it now has nothing to open: the tablet keeps its own brightness, and everything else works.

Why can Muralis not do this itself?

Brightness is a setting for the whole tablet, so Android asks a person. Being the tablet's admin does not change that. No other part of Muralis needs this permission.
