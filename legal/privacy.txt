Muralis is a kiosk app that shows what you choose on an Android screen you own. This policy says what it does with data, in plain words, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

IN SHORT

Muralis has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours. The developer receives nothing from your panels. What the app stores stays on the device, and what it sends goes only to places you set up yourself. There is one exception, Google's billing library, described under Google Play below.

WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

You are, for your panels, your network, your broker and the services you connect. If a panel's camera, microphone or Bluetooth scan is on where other people are, you must tell them.

The developer handles personal data only when you write to him: he receives your address and your message, uses them to answer you, and deletes them when you ask. The legal basis is that you asked (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).

WHAT IS STORED ON THE PANEL

- Your settings, the names you give things, your corner-tap combinations and the device identifier the app makes for itself: until you change them or uninstall the app.
- Passwords, keys, certificates and the Pro purchase record: encrypted with the Android keystore, until you uninstall the app.
- Pictures you upload, and small previews of the pictures you browse: until you delete them.
- The latest readings of the panel's sensors and of the services you connect: until the next reading.
- Short histories, such as the app's own log and the last tags and versions it saw: older entries drop off as new ones arrive.
- The cookies and sessions of the pages it shows: as a browser keeps them.

A settings file or setup QR you make in the web admin holds your settings, wherever you save it. Passwords go into the file only if you ask, encrypted with a passphrase you choose. Keep both like a key.

WHAT LEAVES THE PANEL, ONLY WHEN YOU SET IT UP

Nothing leaves the panel until you set up where it goes. When you do, the panel sends data only to the places you chose:

- to your own systems on your network, such as your MQTT broker, your browser through the web admin, and your other Muralis panels: the panel's state and settings, its network address, the address of the page it shows, the readings of the sensors you switched on, the address of a device locked out of the web admin, pictures of the screen or the camera when you ask for them or switch that on, and, from the panel that bought Muralis Pro, Google's receipt for the purchase, which the other panels of that fleet keep encrypted to check the fleet's Pro;
- to the pages, cameras and other addresses you enter, which receive the requests the panel makes to them, as from a browser;
- to devices on your network that you let control the panel, for example to play a sound.

Your broker keeps the latest status until you clear it. MQTT is not encrypted unless you switch on TLS; connections between your panels are always encrypted. The full content of each message is listed on the API page. None of it is used for analytics, advertising or profiling.

OUTSIDE SERVICES YOU CONNECT

When you connect Muralis to an outside service, such as a weather service, a calendar or a monitoring tool, the panel calls that service on its own schedule, with the account, key or link you entered. The service receives what that call needs, such as a place, a link or your key, and the panel's network address. Each service handles this under its own privacy policy. In a fleet, one panel makes the call and passes the readings to your other panels.

SENSORS AND PERMISSIONS

Every sensor that reads the room is off until you switch it on, and Android asks for its permission when it needs one. When a sensor is on, its reading goes into the status message and the web admin, as described above.

- Camera: pictures for the web admin and, if you switch that on, for your broker; nothing is recorded. It is also used to scan a code when you add a panel; that picture is not kept.
- Microphone: only a loudness number. No sound is recorded or sent.
- Location: Android requires it to scan for Bluetooth devices nearby. The app reads no position with it.
- Photos: read only to show the pictures you pick. They are never copied, changed or deleted.
- Installed apps: the list is read only so you can choose one to show.

If you allow it, the page the panel shows can use the camera and microphone, as a website does in a browser; what it does with them is up to that page. Muralis does not read contacts, messages, call history or your Wi-Fi network's name.

GOOGLE PLAY

Google sells Muralis Pro and takes the payment. The developer never sees who bought it or how they paid. The panel asks the Google Play app on the device what the account has bought, and keeps Google's signed answer.

Google's billing library is in every copy of the app, Free included. About once an hour it sends Google a diagnostic log of its own work, also on a device without Google Play: the library and app version, the device's maker, model and Android build, the country and language, the mobile carrier and network type, and the result of each call to Google Play. Google uses it under its own privacy policy. The developer receives none of it, and it carries none of your settings, addresses or pictures.

DELETING

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes everything it stored. Copies on your broker you clear there.

CHILDREN

Muralis is not directed at children. It has no accounts, profiles or messages, and asks nothing about the person using it.

YOUR RIGHTS

The GDPR gives you the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, move and object to personal data someone holds about you. For everything the app handles, that someone is you, on your own devices and services. The developer holds only the mail you send him, and deletes it on request. You may also complain to a data protection authority; for the developer that is the Austrian one, dsb.gv.at.
