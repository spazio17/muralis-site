Requirements

What a tablet needs to run Muralis.

Android
8.0 (API 26) or newer.
Memory
2 GB recommended. Muralis itself needs little, the page you show uses most of it.
Processor
Any processor that runs Android 8, 32 or 64 bit.
Full kiosk
Device owner, by QR at setup or over adb. Without it, Muralis still runs and recovers its page, with the system bars reachable, but does not reopen itself after a reboot.
Android TV
A TV box runs Muralis too, driven by its remote: Back held for three seconds, or Menu, opens the settings. Where it cannot be made the device owner, it runs as an ordinary app.
Google account
Only to buy Pro. The other panels of the fleet need none.
