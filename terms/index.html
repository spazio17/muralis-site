Muralis Pro

Everything one panel does is free and stays free, on every panel you own, with no limit on how many. Each free panel stands on its own.

Muralis Pro is a one time purchase for the fleet: up to six panels managed together from any of them, with no cloud and no server of ours. It belongs to the Google account that bought it, not to a device. Panels without a Google account take part through the panel or phone that holds the account: each time that device confirms the purchase, Pro stays on the other panels for thirty days, so it must be reachable on your network at least once a month. It does not expire and there is nothing to renew. It cannot be transferred, resold, or shared with another account.

A panel without Pro is not nagged, not watermarked, and not degraded: it does everything on its own, and it can still be added to a fleet that has Pro.

What the app does to the device

Muralis is designed to take over a device. When granted device-owner status it becomes the home app, keeps the screen on its own task so other apps cannot be reached, turns off the system's ways around the kiosk (such as the notification shade, the lock screen and starting in safe mode), allows only the system's own keyboards and accessibility services besides the keyboard in use, keeps the screen on while charging, and grants itself the permissions its features use. Each feature still stays off until you switch it on.

This is the intended behaviour of a kiosk, and you should understand it before provisioning a device you rely on for anything else.

Getting back out

There is always an escape route, and it does not require a cable: a series of corner taps that you record at the first start releases the kiosk and returns the system launcher. The combinations are shown in the app's settings. Change them if you like, and remember what you changed them to.

Removing device-owner status

Device-owner status cannot be granted to an already-configured device, and removing it may require a factory reset. Provision deliberately.

Remote control

Muralis can be controlled from your network: through its web administration, protected by the password you set, and through the other channels you switch on, such as MQTT, protected by your broker. Anyone who passes these checks can change what the panel shows and restart it, so choose a strong password, keep your broker's access tight, and do not reuse a password you use anywhere else. The web administration uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and never takes the password over an unencrypted one: while it cannot use its certificate, for example after a restart until the device is first unlocked, it stays closed. MQTT is unencrypted unless you switch on encryption (TLS) for a broker that offers it. Use these channels only on a network you trust, and reach them over a VPN rather than by opening a port to the internet.
