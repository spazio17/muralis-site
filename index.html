    No cloud. Each panel on its own.

    Every panel is complete by itself. It loads your dashboard straight from your own server and talks only to the things in your home.

    • Free on every panel, with no limit
    • Any web page, Home Assistant too
    • No account, no registration
    • Keeps working when the internet is down
    See the price

    Muralis is the server

    Up to six panels become one fleet, managed from the web admin of any of them. They trust each other by their own keys: nothing to install, nothing to register, nothing in a cloud.

    • Share the page, MQTT, the web admin, the display, Ambient and the alerts, with all or with a group
    • One address for all: {panel.name} gives each panel its own page
    • Alerts when a panel goes offline or its page fails
    • A new panel joins by its camera, or by typing a code
    See the price

    Every location, one page

    Coming after Pro: many screens in many places, each place its own fleet, all on one page. The logs and the numbers go to your own servers.

    • Up to 99 screens, in as many places as you have
    • A dashboard builder for your rooms and receptions
    • Logs and metrics to your own servers
    • Still no cloud and no account
    See the price

    Two ways onto a tablet

    Only the QR makes a locked panel. Google Play is for trying it.

    By QR, the full panel

    Muralis owns the tablet and keeps it on your page. A reset tablet, six taps on its welcome screen, one scan: thirty seconds.

    From Google Play, to try it
